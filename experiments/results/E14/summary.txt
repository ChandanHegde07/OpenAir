RANKING-SAFETY CAVEAT:
  The signal concentrates in the recent-taxi-behaviour rolling stats (s_taxi_mean/med/p90/std).
  Those features consume OTHER departures' TAXITIME, which ranking.parquet BLANKS for DEP.
  They are therefore valid for research fits on training holdouts but CANNOT be computed
  on the real submission input. A ranking-safe variant (rolling MVT-AOBT instead of taxi)
  must be tested before any of this transfers to a submission pipeline.
